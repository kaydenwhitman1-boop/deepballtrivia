Deep Ball is a fan-made game, not affiliated with the NFL or Jomboy Media.
A new three-category board drops every day at midnight Eastern.

How to play

  1. Each round gives you an NFL stat leaderboard — like career rushing yards.
  2. Name players who are on it. Every correct name flips its tile.
  3. Deeper = more points. Your points equal the player's rank: #1 is worth 1, #20 is worth 20.
  4. Name someone who isn't on the list and that's a strike. Three strikes ends the round.
  5. The Daily board has three categories, always including one era challenge (like the 1990s or 2010s), and resets at midnight ET. Free Play is unlimited.

Last names work when they're unique on the board (e.g. "Dickerson"). Small typos are forgiven.
